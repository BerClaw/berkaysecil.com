What I believe

  1. The best time to join a category is before anyone agrees it's one.
  2. A new market doesn't reward the person with the playbook. It rewards whoever figures it out and is right.
  3. Ecosystem growth is a funnel, and launched products are the only number that counts.
  4. Frontier products get sold on education before they get sold on features.
  5. Software was built for people clicking. The next users are agents calling other agents.
  6. Fewer, better shots beat volume. That's true in hiring and in growth.
